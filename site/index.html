Prismtrade LLC / Helios

A clear view of your workspace.

Helios is a read-only workspace client in development. This is its home for product information, privacy, and support.

Not publicly available yet.
There is no App Store download at this time. These pages describe the current implementation, not a promise of future features.

What Helios does today

Pair with a compatible, self-hosted server to retrieve your authorized company workspace. Browse chat metadata, read history when supplied by the server, and view approval records.

The current app does not send messages, run agents, execute work, or offer an AI conversation feature. Approval records are not evidence that an action occurred.

Your server, your workspace

Your administrator supplies the server address and a single-use pairing credential. The app requires HTTPS with a valid certificate. The included local backend is a development foundation; secure ingress and real-device verification are still required before deployment.

Workspace data stays subject to your server operator’s access and retention practices. Disconnecting revokes a device session; it does not delete your organization’s records.
